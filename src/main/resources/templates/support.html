Clinical Boundaries & Scope

No Therapy or Diagnosis: MindMate is an educational, habit-tracking web tool. It does not provide psychological counseling, psychotherapy, psychiatric diagnosis, or medical treatment.
No Emergency Monitoring: This software does not monitor messages in real time or alert university officials, police, or emergency dispatchers.
Safety Heuristic: If keywords related to self-harm or danger are typed, the system immediately provides emergency helpline information.
🛡️

Your Data & Privacy

  • Stored Locally: All check-ins, journal notes, chat conversations, and goals are persisted in your local SQLite database file (mindmate.db) on your computer.
  • No Sensitive Profiling: We do not ask for or collect sensitive clinical history, biometric data, or identity documents.
  • Export Anytime: You can download all your check-in records in standard CSV or JSON format on the History page.
  • Deletion Rights: You can delete any individual entry or wipe your entire history at any time.
